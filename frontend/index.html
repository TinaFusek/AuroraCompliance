How to read this: the law itself is the graph. Each Article of the AI Act was decomposed into Obligations — concrete things someone must do. Every obligation is connected to the Role it binds (provider, deployer…), the RiskCategory that triggers it, its Exceptions, the Deadline when it starts to apply, and the Sanction for breaking it. The glowing lines are the connections the agent actually followed to build the answer — so you can audit its reasoning instead of trusting a black box.
